1. Startup, Session Recovery & Format Selection

Automatic Crash & Session Recovery

Every action you take automatically saves tournament state to rdvccc_state.json in the application directory. If the computer restarts or the program is closed mid-event, launching the app displays the Session Recovery prompt:

  • Resume Saved Event Restores all registered players, payment statuses, table locks, active pods, scores, and round history.
  • Start New Event Clears previous tournament data and starts fresh in the Registration Phase.

Choosing Your Tournament Format

Before starting Round 1, choose your format using the toggle on the top toolbar:

  • Standard Commander Pods (👥 2HG Mode: OFF): Places players into 4-player Commander pods (automatically creating 3-player fallback pods when needed, or a single 5-player pod if exactly 5 players register).
  • Two-Headed Giant (👥 2HG Mode: ON): Pairs teams of 2 into head-to-head (2v2) table matches and enables the Team # column on the Player Manager.

Built-in Demo Modes (For Testing & Training)

  • Demo 1 Loads 36 sample players (including RDV-tagged staff) into the Registration Phase.
  • Demo 2 (Post-Event) Simulates all 3 rounds and jumps straight to the finalized Prizing & Wheel tab.
  • Demo 3 (2HG Ready) Enables 2HG Mode, loads 36 players, and pairs them into 18 teams ready for Round 1.

2. Player Registration & Management (🧑‍💼 Player Manager)

Registering Players

  1. Click the Register Player(s) text box on the top toolbar.
  2. Type a single player name or a comma-separated list of names (e.g., Alice Smith, Bob Jones, Charlie Brown*).
  3. Press Enter or click + Add.
★ Auto-Tagging Staff (RDV Tag): Append *, RDV, or (RDV) to a name during registration (e.g., Eve Adams* or Eve Adams (RDV)) to automatically tag them as [RDV]. RDV-tagged players are automatically marked as Paid and are excluded from the Top 3 Podium and Random Prize Wheel.

Multi-Selection & Column Sorting

  • Single Click: Selects an individual player row.
  • Ctrl + Click Toggles individual players in or out of your current selection.
  • Shift + Click Selects a continuous range of players between two rows.
  • Ctrl + A Selects all players in the list.
  • Sorting Columns: Click any column header (PAYMENT, PLAYER NAME, TEAM #, STATUS, POINTS, OPP PTS (TB), W - L - D) to sort ascending or descending.

Player Manager Controls & Right-Click Actions

Button Shortcut / Trigger Description
✓ Mark Paid Double-click row, Toolbar button, or Right-click Toggles between ✓ Paid and ⏳ Unpaid. Works in bulk when multiple players are selected.
+ Late Player Toolbar button Registers a late-arriving player after Round 1 has started so they are included in the next round.
✎ Edit Name Toolbar button or Right-click Renames a single selected player and updates their name across all active pods, teams, and historical rounds.
🔒 Lock Table Toolbar button or Right-click Locks selected player(s) (and their 2HG partner) to a specific table number (e.g., 1 for Table 1). Enter 0 or leave blank to clear the lock.
★ RDV Tag Toolbar button or Right-click Toggles [RDV] status on selected player(s) and automatically marks them Paid. RDV marked players are ineligible for prizing places on the podium or prize wheel
✕ Drop Player Toolbar button or Right-click Marks selected player(s) as Dropped. Their points remain valid for previous opponents' tiebreakers, but they will not be seated in future rounds.

3. Two-Headed Giant Team Builder (👥 Manage Teams)

When 👥 2HG Mode: ON is active, the 👥 Manage Teams button appears on the Player Manager tab.

  1. Click 👥 Manage Teams to open the Two-Headed Giant Team Builder window.
  2. In the top Available Unassigned Players grid, click the first player (their button highlights blue with a ✓), then click their partner to form a 2HG team.
  3. Table Lock Inheritance: If either player already has a table lock, the entire team automatically inherits that table lock.
  4. Splitting a Team: During registration (before Round 1 starts), click Split Team next to any created team to return both players to the available pool. Splitting a team automatically clears any table locks from both players.

4. Running Rounds & Managing Pods (🫛 Active Pods)

Starting & Advancing Rounds

  • Click ▶ Start Round 1 (Random) on the top-right toolbar to generate Round 1 pairings.
    • Round 1: Randomly seats unlocked players/teams while anchoring any 🔒 Lock Table players/teams to their assigned table numbers.
    • Rounds 2 & 3 (Swiss): Sorts active players/teams by Points and Opponent Points (Tiebreaker), then optimizes pods to avoid repeat opponents while keeping locked players at their fixed tables.
  • ↻ Re-Pod Round Reshuffles the current round's pairings (available before match results are recorded for the round).
  • 📺 Show TV Pairings Opens a maximized, high-contrast pairings window for a secondary TV or projector screen. It updates live whenever seats are swapped or results are recorded.

Manual Pod Adjustments (Swap & Move)

While a table is marked ● IN PROGRESS, you can manually adjust seating:

  • Swap (↔): Click ↔ next to the first player or 2HG team (it highlights blue as ✓ ↔), then click ↔ on a player or team at another table to swap their places.
  • Move (M): Click M to open a dialog and move that player or team to another incomplete table (or to a New Table in 2HG mode).
★ 2HG Automatic Bye Pod: If there is an odd number of active teams in 2HG mode, one team is placed into the ★ BYE POD panel and automatically awarded +3 points (+1 win). You can still use the Swap (↔) button on the Bye Pod to swap another team into the Bye slot—the 3 points automatically transfer to the newly swapped Bye team.

Recording Match Results

  • Player / Team Win: Click Won (+3) next to the winner to award +3 points (+1 Win) to the winner and log a Loss for opponents.
  • Pod / Match Draw: Click 🤝 Record Pod/Match Draw (+1 pt) at the bottom of the card to award +1 point (+1 Draw) to all participants at that table.
  • Correcting Results (↺ Undo): On any completed table card, click ↺ Undo to revert the points and re-open the table.

5. Prizing, Standings & TV Wheel (🏆 Prizing & Wheel)

Once all tables in Round 3 have results recorded, the tournament finalizes and unlocks the prizing controls.

Top 3 Podium & Full Final Standings

  • Top 3 Podium Cards: Automatically displays the 1st Place, 2nd Place, and 3rd Place eligible players or 2HG teams based on Points and Opponent Points (Tiebreaker), automatically skipping any [RDV] staff tags.
  • 🏆 View Full Final Standings Opens a maximized leaderboard displaying Rank, Player/Team Name, Points, Opponent Points (Tiebreaker), and W-L-D record for everyone in the event.

Running the Synchronized TV Wheel

The Prize Wheel automatically includes all active, non-[RDV] players or teams who did not finish in the Top 3 Podium.

  1. Click 📺 TV Wheel to launch the 1080p-scaled, view-only Podium and Prize Wheel window on your secondary TV or projector screen.
  2. In the main control window, click 🎰 SPIN PRIZE WHEEL.
  3. Both the control wheel and the TV Wheel spin in sync for 7 seconds, pause on the winning slot, and display a celebratory winner banner for 5 seconds.
  4. Each winner is recorded under Last Winner and removed from the wheel pool so they cannot be drawn twice. Click 🎰 SPIN AGAIN for additional door prizes.

6. Night's Matchups & Plain-Text Export (📋 Matchups & Winners)

Viewing All Matchups & Winners

Click 📋 Matchups & Winners on the Prizing & Wheel tab at any point during or after the tournament to open a multi-column summary showing every table matchup and winner from Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3.

Exporting the Night's Report to Plain Text (.txt)

  1. Open the 📋 Matchups & Winners window.
  2. Click 📄 Export to Text (.txt) in the top-right corner of the window.
  3. The Save dialog automatically pre-fills the filename as Commander FNM ddmmyyyy.txt (using today's date, e.g., Commander FNM 28102026.txt).
  4. Click Save to write a clean, formatted plain-text report containing:
    • Event header, date, and format (Commander Pods or 2HG).
    • Every round's table matchups, winners, draws, and byes.
    • Complete ranked standings with Points, Opponent Points, and W-L-D records.
    • All Random Prize Wheel winners drawn that night.
